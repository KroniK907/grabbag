Judge

One seated player is the judge each round. The judge draws a prompt and does not submit an answer. After everyone locks, the judge reveals the plays and picks a winner.

Submit

Everyone else picks cards from their hand to fill the holes under the prompt, then locks. Locked plays stay face down until the judge reveals them.

Pick holes

Some prompts need more than one card. Fill every hole. Tap a slotted card to put it back. Lock stays off until the stack is full.

Reveal

The judge flips one play at a time, then confirms a winner. Names show after that pick.

How you win

Score points when the judge picks you. Reach the score target, or finish the scheduled rounds, and the highest score wins.

{{if .Skip}}

Skip

After the draw, only the judge sees the prompt. Players wait until the judge locks it in or skips. A skip draws another prompt and is not a round.

{{end}} {{if .Multi}}

Two prompts

The judge sees two prompts and chooses one. The unused prompt goes back in the pile.

{{end}} {{if .Voting}}

Favorites

After a play is revealed, you may vote for a favorite. Favorite points are separate from the judge winner. You cannot vote for yourself.

{{end}} {{if .Wildcard}}

Wildcard

You get one blank extra card. Type an answer and slot it like any other card. Bots and the judge do not get a blank.

{{end}} {{if .Multiplier}}

Last round

When someone hits the score target, one more round may pay extra points. A lead tie after that is sudden death at the same multiplier.

{{end}} {{if .Timers}}

Timers

A timer may auto-lock a hand or auto-reveal the next play. Opening this page does not pause those timers.

{{end}} {{end}}
