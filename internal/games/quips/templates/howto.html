How to play

Back to game Board

Write

Write a short answer for every prompt on your phone. Finish every answer, then lock them in.

Vote

Each pair of answers appears on the board. Pick your favorite on your phone. You cannot vote for your own answer unless the host allows it.

Score

Votes award points. Later rounds may multiply those points. The player with the highest final score wins.

Last Quip

When enabled, everyone answers the same final prompt. All answers compete in one vote.
