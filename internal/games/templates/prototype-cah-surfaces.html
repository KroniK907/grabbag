Which visual direction best fits the picker, TV board, and player phone surfaces for Apples for Humanity?

Locked. Neon cabinet is the official Apples for Humanity look, light and dark. Dark stays host neon-dark, cyan #19e3ff. Light cyan is #0ab8d4 on both this prototype and host neon-light, so cabinet buttons match the game.
