Write

Everyone seated writes two true facts about themselves and one lie. Pick truths that are a little weird and that most of the room does not know.

Tell

One player at a time reads a card about themselves. It might be their own truth, a lie, or a true fact that belongs to someone else on the couch. They have to claim it either way. Only the teller's phone says which.

Ask questions and vote

Ask the teller questions out loud, then vote True or Lie. If you think it is a lie, say what kind: a pure lie, or someone else's truth. If it was borrowed, guess whose truth it is.

{{if .KnewIt}}

I already know this

Already know the answer? Tap I already know this while the card is read and pick the answer privately. Right gets you 1 point, wrong gets you nothing, and you sit out the vote. If {{.Void}} the room already knows it, the card is void.

{{end}}

This Is My

Everyone may upload one photo they took. The board shows a photo and three players, and all three claim they took it. Only one did, or now and then, none of them did. Each claimant tells the room about it, the room asks them questions, then votes for a claimant or None of them. Your phone tells you privately if the photo is yours.

Score

Right on True or Lie is 2 points, plus 1 for the right kind of lie. Naming the owner is 2. The teller scores 1 for each voter fooled. The owner of a borrowed truth, or the author of a lie, scores 2 if the room did not catch them. The teller can give either of them 1 more for helping sell it. In This Is My, the right claimant is 2, right on None of them is 3, and each claimant scores 1 per vote they got.

{{end}}
